# Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Payment Aggregator Compliance

## 1. Regulatory Context

Under Section 18 of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007 (PSSA), entities operating payment aggregation services in India must obtain formal authorization from the Reserve Bank of India.

The primary regulatory circular is **RBI/DPSS/2019-20/174 DPSS.CO.PD.No.1810/02.14.008/2019-20** ("Guidelines on Regulation of Payment Aggregators and Payment Gateways").

## 2. Key Compliance Mandates

1. **Capital Adequacy**:
   - Minimum net worth of ₹15 Crores at the time of application, stepping up to ₹25 Crores within 3 financial years.

2. **Escrow Account Maintenance**:
   - Customer payment funds must be held in an Escrow Account with a designated scheduled commercial bank.
   - Zero commingling of platform funds with merchant settlement balances.

3. **Merchant Due Diligence (MDD)**:
   - Verification of Merchant PAN, GSTIN, Bank Accounts (penny-drop verification).
   - Verification of Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) > 25% (or > 15% for partnerships).
   - Screening against AML/CFT sanctioned lists.

4. **Card Data Security**:
   - Zero storage of raw credit/debit card numbers or CVV.
   - Enforcement of RBI CoF (Card-on-File) Network Tokenisation guidelines.
